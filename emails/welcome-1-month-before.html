One month until Lost in the Grass — hi from the team. Updates coming soon.

21–23 August 2026

 

Hi[[data:vorname:"there"]],

One month to go until Lost in the Grass — and we can’t wait to see you in the fields near Malvern. Here’s everything you need to help you plan.

Site opening times and camping

The site is open from 12:00pm Friday to 12:00pm Monday, and you’re welcome to camp for the whole weekend. The gate is open for arrivals from 12:00pm–6:00pm on Friday and 10:00am–6:00pm on Saturday. If you’re arriving outside these times, please let us know so we can make arrangements.

Parking with tents

You can park right next to your tent, but for everyone’s safety this can only be arranged between 10:00am–6:00pm. If you arrive outside these hours, please use the car park and move your vehicle to your pitch the next day.

Schedule

The full lineup and wellness schedule will be confirmed very shortly — we’ll email you the moment it’s ready.

Cafe & food options

We’re busy putting the finishing touches to the cafe menu, and it’ll be on the website soon. We do recommend bringing some food of your own for outside the cafe’s opening hours, and we’ll let you know as soon as the menu is live.

Yoga

We’d be delighted for you to join one of our free yoga sessions. We have some mats available, but as they’re subject to availability we’d recommend bringing your own if you can.

Kids disco

The kids can get their groove on at our disco on Friday at 5:00pm. We’d love your little ones (if you have them) to hear their favourite tracks — just reply to this email with their top picks and we’ll add them to the playlist.

For more tips and answers to common questions, take a look at our Good to know section on the website.

See you in the grass,
The LITG team
